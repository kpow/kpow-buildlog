Second round on the brain:

- **Web search:** Claude's server-side search, two searches at most. About 4¢ an answer. Only the text before the first search and after the last gets spoken, and the "let me check" line plays the moment a search starts.
- **Music:** what's playing, play/pause/next, volume, and playing a Spotify link it found with search. It goes through vizMac.
- **Voice:** replies use Kokoro-82M, running locally on the Mac's CPU, instead of macOS `say`. `/v1/say` speaks exact text on the bot with no model call.

On the firmware side: auto-reconnect was off, and the boot attempt was the only retry, so a router blip at night left Stackchan offline until someone power-cycled it. A watchdog now rejoins after 30 s down, backing off to every 5 minutes. A forced drop came back in 70 s.
