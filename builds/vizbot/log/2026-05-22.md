vizBot 3.0 put the face on a StackChan body. Built up in phases: a minimum-viable StackChan build target, StackChan hardware driver bring-up, web endpoints with pitch safety clamping, then servo expressivity, touch reactions, idle sweeps, and a chill mode. Replaced the old ambient effects with 16 patterns ported from noodle-v2. Had to pin M5GFX@0.2.19 — 0.2.21 gave the Core S3 a black screen.
