vizBot 3.0: the face got a body. A StackChan base, brought up in phases from a minimum-viable build target through driver bring-up, web endpoints with pitch clamping for safety, and then the parts that make it feel alive, servo expressivity, touch reactions, idle sweeps, and a chill mode.

Swapped the old ambient effects for 16 patterns ported from NoodleLab.

> **Trap:** pin `M5GFX@0.2.19`. Version 0.2.21 gives the Core S3 a black screen, and nothing about the symptom points at the graphics library.
