vizMini is now Vizzy, and he's close to final. He has a new striped translucent body with a "vizzy" wordmark in Slackey on the back.

- **Case:** a 7 mm switch hole on top of his head, and the back cover's screw holes opened up to 3.4 mm for M3x8 screws with 7 mm heads.
- **Lights:** the strip grew from 8 to 12 LEDs, 4 down each side and 4 along the bottom by his pants. The patterns treat them as one U round the body, so waves and heartbeats climb from the pants up both sides and chases run all the way round. Two new patterns, rise and sides, are built on that shape.
- **Button:** moved to GPIO3. A press changes his expression and jumps the lights to a new pattern and palette.
- **Clock and weather:** the screen sits deep in the body and its edges were hard to see, so both views now sit inside a border with the text centred underneath.
