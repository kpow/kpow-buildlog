The bot's page has a Speaking voice list in the Brain (voice) card. It holds all 28 English Kokoro voices plus five mixes, like Old British gentleman (George and Lewis, slowed down). Picking one makes him say a sample line in it. The page goes through the bot to reach the brain, so the pairing token never reaches the browser.

> **Trap:** every British Kokoro voice spoke with American pronunciation, because the language was hardcoded to en-us. Mostly British voices now get en-gb.

Connectors, answering back, covers and the voice picker shipped as vizbrain 1.2.0 and firmware v3.7.35. The cover flash on every song and the face in the corner followed the same night as 1.2.1 and v3.7.36. spotify-mcp is public on GitHub.
