I wanted to sit with skunky and ask him how he works. Now he looks it up. A new connector, vizself, hands vizBot Brain his own code. It reads the vizpow repo from git at the newest firmware tag, so it describes what's flashed on him, not whatever branch I have checked out. Search mixes keywords with a small local embedding model and a self-map of every file, each with the spoken questions it answers.

- **Code mode:** while he digs, his face rounds into a big ink drop over a dim scrolling code minimap. Two mirrored tentacles read along the lines, his eyes scan them, and patterns stack on his head. Two of them are new: a "compile" progress bar and an "aha" finger.
- **Quips:** every 8 seconds or so he stops reading and says something corny into the same reply clip. That also keeps the bot's 45 second read timeout from ending a long dig.
- **Found it:** the line lights up, both tentacles grab its ends, a Grand Crown bursts with two droplets, and he turns back into his face to answer.

> **Trap:** in the first voice test he answered from his own prompt instead of looking anything up, because "use tools only to change things" won. He also narrated between look-ups. The prompt now makes questions about himself the exception, and anything said between look-ups is held back.

It costs 14 ms a frame on the CoreS3, twice the estimate, because the canvas lives in PSRAM. The frame rate didn't move. A 10-question test came back 10 for 10. Out as firmware v3.10.0 and vizbrain 1.4.0, on skunky.
