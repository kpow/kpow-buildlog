Both firmwares learned bot protocol v2 for the rebuilt VizCloud. It's additive, so a v2 bot still works against the old server.

- **Identity:** each bot gets its own token on register and keeps it in NVS. A refused token used to stop cloud calls until a reboot. Now the bot backs off an hour, drops the token and registers again.
- **Names:** the bot reports its own device name on every sync. A rename in the admin comes down as config and goes through the same path as the web panel.
- **Settings:** brightness, personality and poll interval arrive the same way, with no reboot.
- **Cloud OTA:** the image downloads to the inactive slot, and its size and SHA-256 are checked before switching. It's kept only after a successful sync, otherwise it rolls back. Never during a web upload or on a low battery, and at most hourly.

vizMini 0.8.0.1 got its own small client with a 2 KB reply cap. Its cloud updates need 80 KB of free heap and get a 10 minute rollback window. Weather, cloud calls and web uploads now take turns, because the C3's heap can't hold two at once. Each unit also got its own device name, defaulting to vizMini plus four hex digits.
