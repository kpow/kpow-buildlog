Built the face engine in phases: core face with 12 expressions, then sayings and speech bubbles, then personality presets and a full web UI, then 20 expressions with backgrounds. Everything is drawn from primitives so it scales with the screen. Split the firmware in two — vizpow keeps the effects, vizbot gets the face — and renamed the WiFi AP from VizPow to vizBot.
