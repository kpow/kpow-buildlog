This is the day it stopped being a matrix controller.

Built the face engine in phases: a core face with 12 expressions, then sayings and speech bubbles, then personality presets with a web UI, then 20 expressions with backgrounds. Everything is drawn from primitives, so the face scales to whatever screen it lands on instead of being a pile of bitmaps per device.

Then split the firmware in two. vizpow keeps the effects, vizbot gets the face. The AP name changed from VizPow to vizBot, which is the moment I admitted what I was building.
