A LiPo cell went into the 1.69 for a pendant version. On battery the board's PWR button only feeds it while the button is held.

The firmware now drives SYS_EN (GPIO41) high at the top of setup(), which keeps the board on after the button is released. Two ways to turn it off again:

- **PWR button:** hold for 2 s.
- **Touch UI:** the Power off hold row in System.

Both save settings, show "Goodbye!", then drop the latch. On USB the latch can't cut power, so the bot restarts instead. Shipped as v3.7.18.
