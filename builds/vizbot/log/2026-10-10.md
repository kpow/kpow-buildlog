The 8-LED ring in the 1.69 was swapped for a 4x4 WS2812B panel, then a 5x5 the same evening. Wiring is unchanged: GPIO17, powered from 5V.

A grid can show the picture, which the ring couldn't. Screen mode now splits the drawn frame into 25 patches and gives each LED the average colour of its patch. Averaging, not one pixel per LED, is there to keep fine stripes from strobing. The ring's 7 motions aren't used on the panel.

The panel is wired zig-zag, and the other modes walk that chain. Going from 4x4 to 5x5 was two numbers in the config. 25 LEDs update in 0.57 ms. Shipped as v3.7.15.
