Forget removes a bot and everything tied to it. The next time that bot syncs, the cloud answers approval "unknown". v2 firmware (vizBot 3.11.2, vizMini 0.8.1) then drops its id and token and registers as a new bot waiting for approval. That gives the old bots a clean start on the new server.

Three real bots went through the whole loop: update, forget, re-register, approve, get content. They were skunky the StackChan, a 1.69 inch LCD bot and a vizMini. The 1.69 and the vizMini updated over cloud OTA.

The database migrations were rehearsed against the real production schema first and passed 11 of 11 checks. The first deploy rebuilt the old commit after a settings change and failed its health check without harm. The second shipped the merged code.

> **Next:** forget all bots in production and re-approve the fleet as each one comes back.
