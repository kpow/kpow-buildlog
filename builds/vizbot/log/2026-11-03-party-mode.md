Party mode on vizMini is the other bots' version turned inside out: the screen goes white and he becomes a black ferrofluid drop. The drop roams the screen, grows symmetric magnetic spike patterns on a turning axis (11 patterns, two can stack) and reaches out with bulb-tipped tentacles. His face's black background morphs into the drop and back.

Visits are automatic: about a minute of party mode every 8 to 15 minutes. It can also stay on, or be switched off.
