Gave it senses:

- **Mic:** an audio-analysis driver, with sound-reactive expressions on top
- **Proximity and light:** reactive expressions, plus auto-brightness
- **Face:** reworked to 6 eye shapes, 6 mouth shapes and 8 new expressions
- **Speech bubble:** proportional font, positioned off the sensor readings

There's a hologram mode now too, mirroring the LCD horizontally for a Pepper's-ghost prism. It is a silly trick and it looks fantastic.
