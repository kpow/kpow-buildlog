Added an emoji mode with a sprite picker, 32 icon sprites, and tooling to manage them without hand-editing arrays.

Effects and palettes now auto-cycle out of a shuffle bag rather than a random pick, so you don't get the same one twice in a row. And the IMU got a job: shake the thing to change mode.
