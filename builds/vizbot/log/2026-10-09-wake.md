Saying "Hey vizBot" now starts a voice turn on the Stackchan, the same as tapping the front of its head. Detection runs on the Mac in vizbrain. The bot streams mic audio over UDP only while the room is louder than its noise floor, with 1 second of pre-roll.

The model is an openWakeWord classifier trained on 47 real "Hey vizBot"s recorded through the bot's mic, plus 7,129 synthetic ones. The first model false-fired about 1,000 times per 10 hours. Ramping the negative weight to 1500 brought that to 6.5, next to 4.7 for openWakeWord's own hey_jarvis on the same test.

The first live try scored 0.99, and the reply started about 1.5 seconds after I stopped talking. Firmware 3.7.13.

> **Trap:** Whisper can't label wake-word recordings. On quiet clips it invents "Thanks for watching!", and prompted with the phrase it hears it in music. A page with a Record button and a script fixed it: one labelled clip per press.
