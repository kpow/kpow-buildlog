Moved vizbot and vizpow off the Arduino IDE onto PlatformIO for reproducible builds and versioned firmware binaries, with bins published to a top-level builds/ directory. Integrated a SAM2695 MIDI synthesizer across several phases, including cloud MIDI sequence caching and sync so the bot can pull tunes down. It plays MIDI now in addition to speech.
