spotify-mcp is a second connector, and it borrows vizSpot's Spotify sign-in. The vizSpot on the same WiFi hands out its token, so play, search, queue and now playing work with no setup. Playlists and likes need permissions vizSpot never asked for, so the Mac signs in once with the same Spotify app. Spotify's February 2026 rules cap a personal app at five people and want its owner on Premium, so a second app was never the plan. Starting a song used to take about 10 s of web searching for a link. Now he starts talking in under 2.

> **Trap:** the new save-to-library endpoint, `PUT /me/library`, ignores a JSON body. It wants the URIs in the query string, and answers "Missing required field: uris" otherwise.

- **Covers:** the brain fetches the cover, makes a 240×240 baseline JPEG with macOS `sips` and posts it to the bot. The bot decodes it once and shows it for 8 s, and never opens TLS for it.
- **Every song:** it also flashes on each song change. That's read from vizSpot's own state, so watching costs no Spotify calls.
- **His face:** stays on, small, in a black circle in the corner.
