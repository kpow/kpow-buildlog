The character art in void, a voice companion for Claude Code, got me wanting better eyes. vizBot had 13 separate eye drawings, and faces swapped halfway through a blend.

The rework happened in an eye lab page: today's renderer next to the new model at real device pixels, with sliders that save. A design agent and an art director agent ran four review rounds against a six-point scorecard, and I tuned seven faces myself.

- **One eye shape:** top and bottom lids, slant, sag and a closed arc. The lids carry the emotion and the pupils stay black cut-outs.
- **Springs:** faces blend instead of snapping, and a blink closes in 80 ms and opens in 140 ms.
- **Morphs:** hearts and stars grow out of the pupil, and the spiral, X and glitch close with the lids.

All 600 face-to-face changes pass in the lab. On the bots a face draws in 4.0 ms on the 1.69 and 6.3 ms on the Stackchan. It's flashed to old-dino and skunky as v3.7.39, with the PR open.
