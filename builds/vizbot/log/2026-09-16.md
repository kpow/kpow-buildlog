New target for a CoreS3 sitting in an M5Stack Faces Bottom3: a gamepad panel and two rows of five SK6812 LEDs. It shares flash with the kFun game console, and a chooser in the factory partition picks between them.

It's deliberately not the Stackchan target. That one assumes servos, head touch and a power monitor without probing, so here it would report a healthy servo stack and then block the loop for about 4.6 s every five minutes hunting for hardware that isn't there. The new flag pulls in the LED effect engine and nothing else, and all ten LED modes run unchanged on the 10-LED strip.

> **Trap:** the strips' data line is GPIO13, which is also the CoreS3's I2S bit clock. Mic, speaker and LEDs can't all run. Audio no longer starts at boot, because just starting the mic filled the strips with garbage.
