The bot mutes his own speaker whenever a clip is the Mac's to play, so a clip the Mac dropped was silence with nothing behind it. The log said he spoke, `/brain/status` said it played, and the speaker toggle on his page reported no problem at all. Three changes so that can't happen again.

- **Fallback:** MacSpeaker keeps its last playback outcome with the time it happened. While that outcome is a failure the clips come out of his own 1 W speaker instead: quiet, but audible. The window lapses on its own, and the clip that goes back to the Mac is the one that re-reads the sound devices.
- **One line per clip:** whether it ran out, was cut short by a head tap or stalled, the seconds handed to PortAudio, the output it actually went to and the wall time it took. A device sitting on the writes shows up as a wall time that doesn't match. Not behind the debug flag, on purpose.
- **`/v1/health`:** the Mac's output volume, its mute switch, and the output a clip would go to right now. A speaker muted at its own button looked exactly like a clip we dropped.

None of it reaches `mac_problem`, which is the string the bot's page renders. That one still reports only what someone has to act on.

> **Lesson:** two mornings went into proving a negative out of a log that read the same whether the audio reached a speaker or went nowhere. The per-clip line is for the morning nobody thought to turn debug on first, which is every morning it's needed.

Shipped as vizbrain 1.3.1. Still wants firmware 3.7.37 for the Mac speaker.
