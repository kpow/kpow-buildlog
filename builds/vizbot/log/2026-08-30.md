Chased the StackChan head stalls to ground, and the chase was the interesting part. The head would seize after hours of running, but the servo bus still answers `ReadPos` during a stall, so my angle-floor watchdog never fired. A day of watching it proved detection was a dead end: the bus isn't deaf, the motion layer just stops producing movement. Meanwhile a manual reinit recovered it every single time, in about a second.

So it stopped trying to be clever. The rail cycles blind every 5 minutes, and the rest of the work was making that cycle not look awful:

- capture the pose *before* cutting power, then glide back to it over ~1.8 s instead of snapping home
- bow to the pitch floor first, over 1.2 s, so the head has nowhere to free-fall when torque vanishes

It reads as a slow bow now, down and back up, rather than a collapse and a recover. Also calibrated the pitch zero and moved onto the M5 StackChan-BSP driver, arming the servos properly with position mode and torque.

Four vizBots are living in other places now, with vizCloud up for pushing them content.
