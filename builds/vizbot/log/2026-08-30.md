Chased the StackChan head stalls to ground: calibrated the pitch zero, moved to the M5 StackChan-BSP driver, armed the servos properly with position mode and torque, and replaced a flaky servo watchdog with a blind 5-minute rail cycle plus a graceful glide back to pose after reinit instead of snapping home. Four vizBots are now running in other locations, and vizCloud is live for adding content.
