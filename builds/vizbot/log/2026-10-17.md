A sustained shake toggles info mode, and its only page is weather. Low power mode never starts the radio, so on the 1.69 that gesture landed on a page with nothing on it. A pendant on a lanyard shakes all day by itself, so it landed there over and over.

With the radio off a shake now rolls one of three reactions instead, weighted 5 / 3 / 2:

- **Dizzy meter:** keep shaking and it winds up through surprised at 0.6 s, dizzy at 1.8 s and glitching at 3.5 s, each with its own word (Whoa, Dizzy, SPINNING) and an LED ring flash in that expression's mood colour. It settles half a second after the shaking stops.
- **Fortune:** one of 16 magic-8-ball answers in the speech bubble.
- **Shuffle:** rerolls the palette and the LED ring mode, and the ambient effect only when the background is already ambient.

Reactions run on a budget so the pendant doesn't perform the whole way down the block: 8 s of quiet between them, 4 per 2 minutes, then 3 minutes where shakes are ignored outright. At full power nothing changes, and a shake still opens weather.

Flashed over USB to the black skull-case 1.69 and it worked as expected. Low power mode is becoming pendant mode, so only the new code carries the name for now. Out as v3.7.38.
