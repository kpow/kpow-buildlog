An 8-LED WS2812B ring on GPIO17 now lights the inside of the 1.69 bot. It runs on the same LED engine as the Stack-chan ring and the Faces strips, as a third backend.

The new default mode follows the screen. A 2D effect can't map onto 8 LEDs pixel for pixel, so the ring copies two things:

- **Colour:** sampled from the frame actually drawn. The background effects build colour from a rotating hue, not the palette setting, so reading the palette would have been wrong.
- **Motion:** each of the 16 effects maps to one of 7 ring motions. Galaxy spins, Scanline sweeps, Snakes become two chasing dots.

When the background isn't an effect, the ring breathes in the face's mood colour. Tapping the face flashes the ring in the colour of the reaction it pulls, then fades back. Settings › Light has Ring brightness and Ring mode, and both survive a reboot. Shipped as v3.7.11.

> **Note:** At full brightness the ring update takes 0.64 ms of a 33 ms frame, and WiFi still joined 5 of 5 boots at 15 dBm.
