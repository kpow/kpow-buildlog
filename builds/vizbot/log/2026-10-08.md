The Stackchan camera driver only runs for a photo now. The DMA channel is released after every stop, so a restart can't leave two channels wired to the camera. Other I2C users wait while the camera sets itself up. TLS buffers moved to PSRAM, which frees about 44 KB of internal RAM per HTTPS connection.

vizBot now renders the same 23 palettes, with the same ids, as the Noodle console, vizSpot and vizMac. The file is generated in the Noodle repo and copied in, so every effect call stayed the same. The old 15 palettes are gone, including 8 custom ones. Saved palette numbers still load but point at different palettes now. Shipped as 3.7.0.
