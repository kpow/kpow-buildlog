Same day, second half: senses.

- **Camera:** the head camera takes a photo and sends it to Claude, so "what am I holding?" works.
- **Arrivals:** motion after a quiet spell counts as someone walking in, and it says hi.
- **Lean-in:** the proximity sensor notices a face up close and it reacts.
- **Memory and routines:** it remembers things you tell it and runs scheduled ones.

> **Trap:** the first arrival test greeted an empty room. A screen turning off changed part of the view. It now ignores cells that always change, needs about a second of sustained motion, and checks a photo before saying hi.
