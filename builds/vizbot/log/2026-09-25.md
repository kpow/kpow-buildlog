The hidden long-press grid is gone. The face is the home screen now: tap to poke it, swipe sideways to change the scene, swipe up for a dock. Gestures are detected in software and tolerate missed reads while the I2C bus is busy.

- **1.69:** dock with Mood, Scene, Weather, Clock, Light and More. The face shrinks into the top half as a live preview. Settings are six category pages with one control per 44 px row.
- **CoreS3, Stackchan, Faces:** the same idea in landscape, with side rails and a master-detail settings page. The rail slot changes by build: Head on Stackchan, Games on Faces, Connect on a plain CoreS3.
- **Web panel:** picking a personality works again, pages render as UTF-8, and the Connected dot turns red when the bot stops answering.

Two dev endpoints, `/debug/screen` and `/debug/touch`, screenshot and drive the UI over WiFi.
