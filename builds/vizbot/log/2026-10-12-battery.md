The 1.69 board already feeds the battery to GPIO1 through a 200k/100k divider, so the pendant got a battery meter with no new parts. A multimeter on the cell read 4.1 V where the firmware read 4.146 V.

- **Top pill:** a 0 to 4 bar battery, or a bolt while a computer's USB is attached.
- **System page:** a Battery row with percent and volts.
- **Cutoff:** under 3.30 V for 10 s on battery, it shows "Battery empty" and drops the power latch.

The first percent scale was a generic LiPo curve. The pendant logged a full run-down to its own flash to replace it: 3 h 11 min with WiFi on. One hour in, with two hours left, the generic curve would have said 9%.

> **Trap:** Nothing on the board stops the cell draining flat. That run went down to 2.6 V and rebooted once there before it died. 3.30 V was 12 minutes from the end.

Two more pendants got cases: one in green stripes, one black with green LED ears.

A second run-down in low power mode is still going. The cutoff has only fired on a faked reading so far. Running on two pendants as v3.7.26, not released yet.
