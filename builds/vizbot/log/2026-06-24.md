The bot in the flesh: neon-green shell, OLED face, StackChan base underneath.

Fixed the servos refusing to answer after a cold boot. They wake up slower than the firmware asks, so the ping retries now until they answer instead of giving up on the first miss. Also decoupled web control from cloud sync, because a slow sync was making the buttons feel dead, and fixed a registration bug that truncated device names.
