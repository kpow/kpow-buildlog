title: "1.69: a touch chip that dozed off, and a reset pin that wasn't wired"

One 1.69 logged an I2C read error about 17 times a second. Another never did.

The touch chip is a CST816T on both. On the noisy board it comes out of reset with auto-sleep on: two seconds after the last touch it drops into standby and stops answering its address until a finger wakes it. The firmware polls touch about 12 times a second, so every idle poll failed. Touch still worked, because touching wakes it. Writing DisAutoSleep after reset keeps it awake.

- **Reset pin:** the firmware pulsed GPIO21, which isn't wired to the chip. The touch reset is GPIO13. Init had only worked through a fallback that tried three pins in turn.
- **Battery pin:** the call that set its attenuation did nothing, so it had always read at the default 11 dB. The curve and cutoff were measured that way, so nothing changed but the comment.

Shipped as v3.7.30.
