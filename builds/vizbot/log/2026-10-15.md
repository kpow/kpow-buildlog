The Stackchan at full volume was too quiet for the room. A Bluetooth speaker on the bot itself can't work: the CoreS3 is an ESP32-S3, which has BLE only, with no Classic Bluetooth and so no A2DP. Turning up the AW88298 amp's boost was out too, since it hangs off the same rail as the servo brownouts.

So his voice goes through the Mac instead. With the new `speaker` setting on `"mac"`, vizbrain plays each reply on the Mac's sound output (sounddevice, which bundles PortAudio), so a speaker paired to the Mac becomes his voice. The bot still fetches the clip and plays it at volume 0, so the mouth keeps the speaker's clock and lines up. A head tap that stops him sends `/v1/hush` so the Mac stops too.

The switch is on the bot's page: Talk through the Mac's speaker, in the Brain (voice) card. Checked by ear on the paired speaker, then shipped as vizbrain 1.3.0 and firmware v3.7.37.

> **Trap:** PortAudio reads CoreAudio's device list once, at startup. The Bluetooth speaker dropped and reconnected, took a new CoreAudio ID, and every open after that failed with PaErrorCode -9986. He went silent on the Mac until vizbrain restarted. MacSpeaker now re-reads the list before each clip, about 3 ms.
