The black pendant's LED sticks need no pin and no code. Each one is a COB filament on a pad with its own driver chip, and all it does is run a chase.

So the wiring is as low tech as it gets. A pair of leads off 3.3 V and G on the 1.69 dev board, with a rocker switch cut into the middle of the 3.3 line. Flip the rocker and the sticks come up: turbo mode.

They are wicked bright.
