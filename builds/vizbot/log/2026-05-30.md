The Si12T head touch was firing phantom reactions, and my zone model was wrong. The three pads run front, middle and back along the top of the head, not left to right.

- **Debounce:** M5Unified's button class instead of a hand-rolled state machine
- **Sensitivity:** raised to match the StackChan BSP reference
- **Reactions:** front tap nods, back tap shakes, each with a random phrase, a synth sound and an LED flash. Hold for 2 seconds and it goes chill.

> **Trap:** only a full-strength hit on the front or back pad counts. Anything less, and stray capacitive coupling, a ceiling fan included, fires reactions.
