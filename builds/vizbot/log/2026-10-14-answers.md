When a reply ends in a question, the bot now beeps and listens for the answer without a tap, up to 3 times in a row. The brain only knows the whole reply after it has answered the bot, so after speaking the bot asks `/v1/listen/<id>` whether to keep listening.

The first live test half worked. "Yeah, go ahead and put on some..." got cut off mid-thought. The next answer, "Reggae.", came back from Whisper as "Ray Gaye", and he went looking for Marvin Gaye.

- **Pause:** answers now wait 1.5 s of silence instead of 0.8 s.
- **Context:** a follow-up is transcribed with his own question in Whisper's prompt. On a synthetic test clip, "Reggae." went from "Rege." to "reggae.".
- **Last sentence:** a question one sentence before the end counts too. "How about some rocksteady? Or the Phish shows could hit the spot." had slipped through.

After that, "Put on some reggae." came through clean.
