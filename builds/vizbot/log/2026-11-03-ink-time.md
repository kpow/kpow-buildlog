vizMini fell back to its hotspot on 4 of 5 boots. The boot logs showed hundreds of join attempts 30 ms apart under the default fast scan, ending in an auth failure that stops the retries. Scanning all channels first joins every time, with an address at about 7 s. `vizmini.local` also took 5 s to resolve until IPv6 went on, so mDNS answers both halves of the lookup.

The clock and weather became Ink Time, ferrofluid ink on a white screen:

- **Clock:** liquid 7-segment digits with magnet spikes that pop each second, drips, and digits that drain and regrow on the minute.
- **Weather:** the temperature in ink, with the drop acting out the sky.
- **Transitions:** face, clock and weather all move through the drop.
- **Boot:** an intro timed to the real WiFi join, ending on the address or the hotspot name.

The control page was rebuilt in the vizBot page style, with a live screen preview, full LED colour controls and a Playground for testing.

> **Trap:** adding wind and rain chance pushed the weather request line past its 256-byte buffer, so Open-Meteo returned a 404 and every field read zero.
