Migrated the LCD target from Arduino_GFX to LovyanGFX; chased a blue tint and dim brightness afterward, and unified canvas management along the way. Built a tween system with 8 easing functions and wired it into the main loop, replacing the hand-rolled smoothstep and linear lerps. Expression transitions and look-around animations now run on the easing curves.
