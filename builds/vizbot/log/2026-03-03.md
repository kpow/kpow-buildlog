Migrated the LCD target from Arduino_GFX to LovyanGFX, then spent the afternoon chasing a blue tint and dim brightness that came with it.

Built a tween system too, with 8 easing functions and wired it through the main loop, which retired a pile of hand-rolled smoothstep and lerp calls. Expression changes and look-around animations ride the curves now, and the difference between a linear blink and an eased one is the whole personality.
