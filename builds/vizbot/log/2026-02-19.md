Wanted the bot's speech to appear on a WLED matrix. Spent days driving WLED's built-in scrolling text effect and fighting it the whole way: scroll speed, trails, jump cuts, and the effect reinitialising between consecutive words.

Gave up on it and went to direct pixel control over DDP, drawing the text myself. Clean frames immediately.

> **Lesson:** when you're fighting somebody else's effect engine for control of individual frames, stop fighting and take the pixels.
