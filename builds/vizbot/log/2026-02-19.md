Added forwarding of the bot's speech to a WLED LED matrix. First approach drove WLED's built-in scrolling text effect; fought it for days over scroll speed, trails, jump cuts, and effect reinit on consecutive words. Switched to direct pixel control over DDP, drawing the text myself, which gave immediate clean frames without relying on the effect engine.
