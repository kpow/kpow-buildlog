Added the Waveshare ESP32-S3-LCD-1.3 as a target, using the board-first config pattern. Hologram mode flips vertically now instead of horizontally, for the Pepper's-ghost prism.

- **Chill:** replaces Sleepy, with half-lidded eyes instead of closed ones
- **Brightness:** fixed the LCD slider and raised the default to full
- **Web UI:** three tabs (Face, Settings, Network) and a neo-brutalist redesign: cream background, thick black borders, hard offset shadows
