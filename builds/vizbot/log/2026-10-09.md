The Waveshare 1.69 has a passive piezo buzzer on GPIO42, and the firmware had never touched it. Waveshare's FAQ says a floating buzzer pin keeps drawing current through the LDO and heats the board. The pin is now held low from the first line of setup, and the board feels cooler.

The CoreS3 sound engine plays on it now, all 37 sequences. A piezo has one voice, so:

- **Lead only:** bass and drum channels are skipped, or they'd chop the melody.
- **Chords:** the top note wins.
- **Range:** anything under G5 (784 Hz) moves up whole octaves, because the piezo is faint down there.

Tapping the face boops. Menu swipes, toggles and buttons each get a sound. After 5 minutes alone it chirps with a matching face, every 4 to 12 minutes, never between 10pm and 8am. Settings › Light has Sound and Volume. Shipped as v3.7.5.

> **Dead end:** I hoped the floating pin explained why the 1.69 can't join WiFi at full 19.5 dBm TX power. With the pin held low it still failed 0 of 6 times at 19.5 and connected 5 of 5 at 15, so it stays at 15.
