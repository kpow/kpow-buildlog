title: "StackChan power and servo fixes: a battery that charges and a head that recovers"
tags: [stackchan, power, firmware]

---
The StackChan's battery never charged. M5.begin's default turns on the CoreS3's 5V output and its boost, and on the StackChan base that keeps USB from reaching the AXP2101. The battery drained at about 0.5 A until the bot crash-looped. The base doesn't use that 5V, so it stays off now.

- **Head freezes:** bench tests showed the stalls are hardware on this unit. Both servos stop answering at once, and only while their motors are driven. A watchdog now pings both every second and cycles the servo rail after 3 missed checks, backing off up to 5 minutes when a recovery doesn't help.
- **Pitch floor:** raised from 25° to 40°. The old floor sat about 4° above the mechanical end stop.
- **Silent hangs:** a wedged web server task left WiFi up and the bot dead, with no coredump. wifi_srv and loop now check in on every pass, and a timer aborts with a coredump if either goes 30 s without one.
- **Internal RAM:** TLS buffers and the 20 KB cloud MIDI table moved to PSRAM. Each TLS handshake had been taking about 30 KB of internal RAM, enough to stop every cloud sync and to leave no room for a photo.

> **Trap:** a WiFi firmware update tripped the task watchdog. The coredump showed the 2 MB image being hashed on core 0 while a cloud TLS handshake ran its ECDSA verify on the same core at the same priority. Cloud syncs now wait out an upload.
