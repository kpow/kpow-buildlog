Once party mode shrank the face into the floating blob, I realized it had cut him off from his speech bubble. Party mode hid every bubble, so talking to skunky through vizBot Brain showed nothing on screen. No "Listening...", no reply, no sign the brain was working.

- **Bubbles:** only bubbles from a vizBot Brain turn come back. Idle sayings stay hidden so the show keeps the screen. The bubble takes the band away from his corner and its tail follows the blob, as the round-one layout spec had already worked out.
- **Staying put:** in the first test he roamed to the other top corner in the middle of a sentence. Now roams wait until the turn is over, and a poke makes him flinch without the dash to another corner.
- **The blob shows the brain:** listening grows an ear tentacle toward the middle of the screen. Thinking runs a slow three-lobe swirl round the rim with a small spike fan circling. Speaking swells the body with the reply's loudness, from the same envelope that drives the mouth.

Out as v3.9.1, on old-dino and skunky.
