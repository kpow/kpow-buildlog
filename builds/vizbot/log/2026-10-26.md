Party mode hands the whole screen to the ambient effect and shrinks the face into a black blob in a corner, eyes still inside. The look comes from void again: a gooey body with a wobbly edge and a shadow under it.

It started as a browser prototype running the eye lab's real eye model at device pixels. A motion design agent specced the springs, spikes and shadow, and a layout agent the sizes and placement. I tried a tiny mouth and the album-art mouth against none and kept none. I dropped the corner setting too, because picking a corner is his job.

- **Body:** a 120-point outline made of breath, three slow edge waves, squash, stretch and a bulge toward his gaze. A rim, a sheen tinted by the show and a floor shadow sit around it.
- **Corners:** a poke sends him dashing to another corner. Every 25 to 60 s he strolls to a new one on his own.
- **Reactions:** a tap elsewhere pulls a fan of spikes toward the finger. A shake turns him into a porcupine.
- **Music:** on the CoreS3 and Stackchan the spikes pump on mic beats. The 1.69 has no mic, so it sends itself a pulse.

Turning it on, the stroke around the eyes becomes the drop under a blink and hops into its corner. It lives under Look in the touch settings and on the web page.

> **Lesson:** the first build cost 6.3 ms a frame on the 1.69. The polygon fill tested all 120 edges on every row, and the rim halved every pixel under the blob before the body covered it. Bucketing crossings by row and filling rim and body in one pass got it to 3.0 ms.

Out as v3.8.0.
