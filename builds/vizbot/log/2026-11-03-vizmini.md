I decided to revisit all my old projects and plus them up: new enclosures, less hacky builds, upgraded software, and some of the AI feel massaged out of them. They're getting ready to be Christmas presents this year, and I only want to give great ones. vizMini, the original vizbot from forever ago, is first.

It's an ESP32-C3 SuperMini with a 1.3" mono OLED. It picked up the eye model from the full vizBot: all 25 eye lab faces, drawn straight onto the 128×64 screen at 0.41 scale so every mouth fits.

- **Face motion:** rare whole-face moves (tilt, wobble, pendulum, drift, spin, double spin, an upside-down flip). They're rotated in fixed point because the C3 has no FPU.
- **Inside lights:** an 8× WS2812B strip on GPIO4 with mood, ambient and spark modes that cycle patterns and palettes.
- **Screen speed:** the OLED had been on bit-banged I2C at about 520 ms a frame. Hardware I2C at 400 kHz brought it to about 33 ms.

> **Trap:** FastLED on the C3 gives one strip both RMT channels, so adding the onboard NeoPixel as a second strip aborted at boot. The onboard pixel stays dark.
