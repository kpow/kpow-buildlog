Stackchan talks now. Tap the front of its head, ask something, and it answers out loud with lip-sync. The thinking runs in vizbrain, a Python app on a Mac: Whisper turns speech into text, Claude writes the reply, and the Mac speaks it back to the bot.

I planned on a middle-pad tap, but a finger on the middle also maxes the front or back pad and fired nod or shake. Front tap it is.

The rest was speed:

- **Streaming:** the bot plays the reply while it downloads (101 ms to first sound), and vizbrain speaks sentence by sentence while Claude is still writing. Typed request to first sound went from 3-4 s to about 2.2 s.
- **Fewer calls:** faces and gestures ride in the reply as tags, so most turns are one Claude call. Prompt caching took a turn from about 0.6¢ to 0.2¢.
