title: "1.69 WiFi: it was the TX power"
tags: [lcd169, wifi]
media: []
---
The Waveshare 1.69 couldn't join WiFi or get an address at full 19.5 dBm transmit power. Receive was fine; the router just never heard it. A TX sweep on two boards connected every time at 15 dBm and failed every time at 19.5, so the 1.69 now runs at 15.

> **Trap:** `WiFi.setTxPower()` silently does nothing if it runs before WiFi has started, and `getTxPower()` then reports 19.5 anyway. The per-board setting had never actually applied. It now calls `esp_wifi_set_max_tx_power()` directly, again after `WiFi.begin()`, and logs the real value.
