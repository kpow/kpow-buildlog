Reworked the scoring engine for fairness. Bustout detection now uses gap data — the stretch
of shows since a song was last played — so calling a rarity is worth real bonus points, and
shared bustouts pay out to everyone who called them. Tightened the exact-position accuracy
scoring, and started surfacing the bonus breakdown on the score page so players can see
exactly why they earned each point instead of trusting a single total.
