Reworked the scoring engine to be fairer about rarity. Bustout detection runs off gap data now, the stretch of shows since a song was last played, so calling a genuine rarity is worth real points. Shared bustouts pay everyone who called it, rather than rewarding whoever saved first.

Tightened exact-position accuracy at the same time, and started showing the bonus breakdown on the score page. If a player can't see why they got the points, the total may as well be random.
