Built Tour Wrapped: a per-player recap of the tour just played, with your best calls, your points and your rarities. Every player gets a personalized link, keyed to the right tour, wired through the email system as a `wrappedLink` variable.

It took a few follow-up fixes to make the callout point at the correct completed tour. Worth it: Wrapped is the thing people actually shared.
