Built a Tour Wrapped stats page: a per-player recap of the tour just played — your best
calls, your points, your rarities. Wired a personalized wrappedLink variable into the email
system so every player gets a direct link to their own recap, keyed to the right tour. It
took a few follow-up fixes to make the callout point at the correct completed tour, and
Wrapped turned out to be the thing people actually shared.
