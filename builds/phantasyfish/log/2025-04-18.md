Closing out the first playable week. Added a random-fill button that populates a setlist
with random songs, for people who want to jump in without building one by hand. Fixed a
batch of setlist-saving bugs that had been dropping songs. Added a check-your-spam-folder
reminder for the account verification email, since sign-ups were coming in and the
verification mail was landing in spam folders.
