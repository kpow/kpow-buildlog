Summer tour meant live mode: a live view that scores predictions in near real time as a
setlist comes in, with an admin side to drive it song by song. Added a compact setlist
display for the live page and pushed bustout callouts right into it, so the leaderboard
reshuffles as each song is entered while the band is still playing.
