WookBot went public: an OpenAI-backed generator that drafts a plausible setlist for an
upcoming show from historical play patterns. Gave it a home-page module showing the show
date and venue, a selector to browse past generations, and skeleton loading states during
generation. It only pulls from published show data, so the suggestions stay grounded in
songs the band actually plays.
