WookBot went public: an OpenAI-backed generator that drafts a plausible setlist for an upcoming show out of historical play patterns. It gets a home-page module with the show date and venue, a selector for browsing past generations, and skeleton states while it thinks.

It only pulls from published show data, so it suggests songs the band actually plays instead of inventing a dream set.
