Whipped up the final case once the white test prints had settled the fit: a black shell with a silver face, a skull badge, and a cover over the two LED panels.

The firmware got a round of polish the same day:

- **Wi-Fi setup from a phone:** the firmware no longer carries any Wi-Fi details. Setup opens the controller's own WPA2 network and shows a QR code for it. The phone's setup page scans it, joins, and reports back. The controller keeps its network and pairing code in its own storage. Tested with a phone.
- **Releases:** the bundled firmware now builds without credentials, and the OTA password is the pairing code.
- **Knob:** turning right now moves right and down.
- **Buttons:** a small high-priority task samples them every 2 ms and queues events, so a quick tap during a screen redraw isn't lost.
- **Effect name:** no more flash of the old name after picking a new one.
