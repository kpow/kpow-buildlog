Phases 5 and 6 in one sitting. The matrix got a player with nine transitions and sixteen ambient patterns ported over from vizpow, then browse, detail, settings and touch calibration screens, then an on-device editor with pencil, fill, picker, eraser, undo and animation frames. Two bugs worth keeping: the LVGL draw buffer needed aligning or the screen came up blank and noisy, and screens had to be freed on leave so the sync task had the fifty-odd kilobytes its TLS handshake wants.
