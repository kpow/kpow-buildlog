Phases 5 and 6, one sitting. The matrix got a player with nine transitions and sixteen ambient patterns ported from vizpow.

Then the screens went in around it: browse, detail, settings and touch calibration. The editor came last, with pencil, fill, picker, eraser, undo and animation frames.

Two bugs worth keeping:

- the LVGL draw buffer has to be aligned, or the screen comes up blank and noisy with no error anywhere
- screens must be freed on leave, because the sync task needs about fifty kilobytes free for its TLS handshake and a leftover screen is exactly what it doesn't have
