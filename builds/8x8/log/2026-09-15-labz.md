Renamed 8x8 Studio to 8x8 labz and redid both front ends neobrutalist: cream paper, black ink, thick borders, hard offset shadows, Slackey headings and lowercase type, with a matching boot title card on the handheld. Split the palette into families of six OKLCH shades, so picking a color on a 320×240 resistive panel is a family dropdown and twelve swatches rather than hunting across a 256-color grid. Home became four big tiles with a live mirror of the LEDs.
