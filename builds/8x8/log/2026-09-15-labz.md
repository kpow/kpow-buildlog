Renamed 8x8 Studio to 8x8 labz, and redid both front ends neobrutalist: cream paper, black ink, thick borders, hard offset shadows, Slackey headings, lowercase everywhere, and a matching boot title card on the handheld.

The palette got split into families of six OKLCH shades. Picking a colour on a 320×240 resistive panel is now a family dropdown and twelve swatches, instead of hunting a 256-colour grid with a fingernail.

Home is four big tiles with a live mirror of the LEDs.
