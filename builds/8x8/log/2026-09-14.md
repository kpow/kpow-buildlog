Built the whole spine in a day: the icon format and its shared library, the C header export, a Postgres-backed API, the catalog and editor and palette pages, and a deploy onto DigitalOcean App Platform. Then scaffolded the firmware and brought up the CYD, with WiFi setup and a first sync landing icons in a LittleFS cache. The panel fought back — colors only came out right as BGR with inversion on, and the ILI9341 has no reset pin, so it keeps its inversion state across warm resets and has to be told again at boot.
