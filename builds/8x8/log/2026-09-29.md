The palettes looked right on the screen and wrong on the matrix. Stepping through a family's shades on the LCD often didn't change the LED at all.

Every color now goes through a calibration on its way to the LEDs: gamma, a gain per channel, and brightness. A new Settings → colors page shows the same test card on the screen and the matrix, with a slider for each. Tuning by eye, I ended at gamma 1.0 with no white balance at brightness 255.

The families were rebuilt for the matrix:

- **Generated for the LEDs:** every shade is snapped to what the LEDs can show, then checked. None may be off, neighbors must differ by at least 3 drive levels, and no two colors may look the same.
- **18 starter families:** 15 hues and 3 whites. Vermilion and indigo landed on red's and blue's levels when dim, and an LED can't show brown or grey.
- **An archive:** colors that existing icons use kept their slots and moved into an archive family, hidden in both editors unless the open icon uses it. Every other slot got a new color. Production's palette is now 155 colors in 19 families.

All three handhelds run the new firmware: two USB-C boards and the first-batch micro-USB board, which still has its matrix on the TX pin.
