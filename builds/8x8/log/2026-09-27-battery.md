The mobile version from the render got printed and powered. A 1000 mAh LiPo sits in a bay at the front of the base, feeding the board through a battery charger board and a rocker switch.

JST connector harnesses hook everything up: battery, charger, switch, board and matrix. The wiring was proven on the bench first, with the matrix running off a JST lead, before any of it went into the case.
