Got the clamshell together. A printed threaded rod runs through a two-part hinge with a knurled head on one end, and there are detents at the knuckle interface so tightening actually holds the angle. Plain chamfers top and bottom, because the curved edges never printed clean and I stopped pretending they would.

Backed out the touch changes I made for the second board. The first board didn't want them.

Then the README, origin story included: a pocket sketchbook where the page is 8×8 and the ink is light.
