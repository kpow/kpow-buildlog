Got the clamshell together: a printed threaded rod through a two-part hinge with a knurled head on one end, detents at the knuckle interface so tightening holds the angle, and plain chamfers top and bottom because the curved edges never printed clean. Backed out the touch changes made for the second board, which the first one did not want. Then wrote the README, origin story included — a pocket sketchbook where the page is 8×8 and the ink is light.
