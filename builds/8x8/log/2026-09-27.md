The new ELEGOO 2.8" USB-C boards rendered black as white. Their ILI9341 wants display inversion off, where the micro-USB boards want it on. Driver and BGR color order are the same, so the panel flags moved into one group per board type, with `elegoo`, `elegoo-tx` and `bringup-elegoo` builds beside the old ones.

> **Trap:** guides call the USB-C variant an ST7789. On this board that driver only gave a white screen.

Dropped the runtime "panel colors" button from the session before. It never worked: it wrote MADCTL through a second, uninitialized TFT_eSPI instance. CN1 (GPIO 27) is wired and working on the USB-C boards.
