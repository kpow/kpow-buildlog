Touch was the day's problem. Moved to pressure-based reads, swapped the four-corner min and max map for a projective calibration, and made the handheld run calibration on first boot instead of shipping the bring-up constants. Also made LED rotation a per-device setting in NVS, since every matrix ends up mounted a different way in a different case. Had the whole thing apart on the mat while I did it.
