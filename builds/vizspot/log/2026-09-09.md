Gave ambient a memory: 20 tracks of history with cached covers, seeded from recently-played at boot and timed against NTP, so a cover you played earlier fades in with how long ago it was. Added the kaleidoscope post-filter with live controls, retuned Puzzle to 16 px tiles and Snakes to a 2× upscale, and restyled the whole control page neo-brutalist with collapsible cards. Also chased a stuck-on-song bug to a token refresh reusing a dead TLS session.
