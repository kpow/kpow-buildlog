A board you hand to someone shouldn't need a computer. So the panel shows a QR code, alternating with a typable base32 card for anyone who would rather not scan:

1. The phone scans it and signs into Spotify on kpow.xyz
2. The credentials come back as an AES-256-GCM envelope the relay can't read
3. The board decrypts, applies, verifies against Spotify, and acks

The first real phone test died on that last step: `ack ok -> http -1`, with the phone sitting on "Board is connecting". The Spotify client was holding the only TLS context again. Now the ack frees the keep-alive before it posts and retries for about 15 seconds. Re-tested on the second board: Welcome back, then You're connected.

The case is finished too. Printed frame and stand, skulls cut into the side panel, and a choice of silver, white, grey or translucent diffusers.
