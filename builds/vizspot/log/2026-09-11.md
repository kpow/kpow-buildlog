Built phone pairing so a board can be given away without a computer: the panel shows a QR code alternating with a typable base32 card, the phone signs into Spotify, and the envelope comes back AES-256-GCM encrypted for the board to decrypt, apply, verify and acknowledge. Forgetting the credentials restarts on a fresh code. The case is done — printed frame and stand, with a choice of silver, white, grey or translucent diffusers.
