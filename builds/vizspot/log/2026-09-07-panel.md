First light came up wrong twice. The board turned out to be a Seengreat RGB Matrix HUB75 S3, not the Waveshare its listing reads like. Then the panel banded red and dropped bits, which looked like a timing fault and was a brown-out on a computer's USB port — its own 5 V supply fixed both, and the budget default ended at 500 mA after a hub browned out at 900. Measured the white balance in linear light, then put the artist and title on the top and bottom edges in a 3×5 face.
