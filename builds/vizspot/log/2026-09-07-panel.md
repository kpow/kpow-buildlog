The board is not what the listing said it was. It reads like a Waveshare ESP32-S3-RGB-Matrix; it is a Seengreat RGB Matrix HUB75 S3, with a different pin map entirely. The giveaway is the module, N16R8 here against Waveshare's N32R16, and `esptool flash_id` will tell you which one you bought.

Then the panel came up banded red with speckled green. I took that for a colour problem and tuned white balance twice, R91 G68 B100, then R20 G38 B98, before doing it properly in linear light. None of it was the bug.

> **Trap:** red with speckled green is a power symptom, not a colour one. Drop brightness to 10%. If the colours come back, it is the supply. Ten seconds, and it saves hours.

It was browning out on a laptop USB port. Its own 5 V supply fixed the colour and the dropped bits together, and the default budget settled at 500 mA after a hub and a second laptop port both browned out at 900. Artist and title went on the top and bottom edges in a 3×5 face once the panel was honest.
