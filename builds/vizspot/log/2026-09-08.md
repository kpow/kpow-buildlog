The thumb wheel kept missing presses and the decoder was not the problem. `loop()` was starved under the visualizer and the FFT, so the poll just wasn't running often enough. Moved it to its own 10 ms key task with latched presses, a rock guard, and a veto so a push doesn't read as a rock.

Three smaller things around it:

- **Status screens:** boot splash, WiFi connecting, setup, unreachable, and the three Spotify states, all redrawn
- **Palettes:** a new random one on every effect change, held per side through the crossfade
- **Brightness:** default up to 100%, ambient uncapped
