Redesigned every status screen — boot splash, WiFi connecting, setup and unreachable, Spotify setup, token and waiting — and made the station retry faster behind the hotspot. Added a random palette on every effect change, carried per side through the crossfade. The thumb wheel was dropping presses because loop() was starved under the visualizer and FFT load, so it moved to a dedicated 10 ms key task with latched presses and a tolerant decoder.
