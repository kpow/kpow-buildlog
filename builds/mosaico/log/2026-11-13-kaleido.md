A morning of polish on how Kaleido looks and moves.

- **Smooth rendering:** the device sampled the 128 px source with nearest texels, so each one showed as a 4-15 px block with stair-stepped edges. It now samples bilinear, with the 240 px render split across both cores as Chladni does it: 33-34 fps, where bilinear on one core managed 27.
- **Steady slow spins:** zoomed in, slow spins stalled and then jumped. Texture coordinates moved to full-width fixed point, which keeps about 20 bits at any zoom.
- **Tuned looks are saved:** edits from Tune, the web sliders or a pinch stay with that look across restarts. The device and page mark it "(edited)", and the Studio page can reset it.
- **Controls:** two fingers sliding up or down step Speed. The L+R chord window doubled to 300 ms. Tilt no longer spins the picture.
- **LEDs:** the four top LEDs sampled the screen quadrants, which a kaleidoscope makes identical. They now sample spots from centre to edge, so the colours differ and flow with the picture.

All motion now runs at 75%: 1.0 felt too fast, 0.5 too slow.

> **Dead end:** rendering at the full 480 px ran at 20 fps and looked no better than bilinear at 240.
