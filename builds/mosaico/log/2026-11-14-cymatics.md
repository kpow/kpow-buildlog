Third app on the Mosaico. The first version was a vibrating drum with a sand
mode, palettes and kaleidoscope folds at 31.8 fps. After trying it I called it
not very cymatics: a lot of Chladni and kaleidoscope. Sand went. Its two most
psychedelic looks, Cathedral and Acid Bloom, moved to Kaleido.

The rewrite treats every pixel as water driven by a tone. The dish's own modes
are Bessel functions with a Neumann wall, and a tone excites the modes whose
resonance (f = 36·k Hz) it sits close to.

- **Looks:** Ring Light, Clear Dish, Sunlit Pool, Mercury, Ink & Light.
- **Scenes:** sweeps slow down and hold on each pattern, then rush through the empty gaps.
- **Tone ruler:** the right edge marks every resonance. Release near a tick and the tone snaps on.
- **Sound:** the ES8311 speaker plays the tone, or the mic listens and a voice drives the water.

After the first hands-on session:

- **Louder:** the tone levels went up to −12 / −6 / −1 dBFS.
- **Taps pluck:** a tap rings the modes that fit the spot instead of dropping a ripple.
- **Pinch zoom:** the default fills the screen, so the dish rim sits off the edge.

It renders 240 px on both cores, doubled to 480, at about 33 fps, the panel's ceiling.

> **Trap:** The Interaction Module's LEDs stayed dark. A health line showed every
> LED write succeeding, then the LEDs died in Kaleido and the stock firmware too.
> A small IC on the module had two legs knocked off. A resolder brought them back.
