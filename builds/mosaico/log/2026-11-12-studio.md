The evening after Kaleido first ran on the device.

- **Studio page:** the phone-only page became a Studio with a desktop layout: Picture on the left and Pattern on the right, like the L and R keys. Phones get tabs and a remote within thumb reach. 18 KB gzipped.
- **Live preview:** the page shows the screen at about 4 fps. Frames go through the S31's hardware JPEG encoder: 9 ms for a preview, 41 ms for a 480×480 snapshot.
- **Two new symmetries:** Snowflake (30-60-90 triangle mirrors) replaced Quilt, and Tunnel (log-polar, flying into the tube) replaced Neon Lattice.
- **Speed:** a new look setting from 0.1x to 3x. Each look got a hidden pace so 1x moves the same amount everywhere.
- **19.5 to 33 fps:** rendering at 240×240 and upscaling 2x with the PPA cut the engine from 46 to about 14 ms. The frame task also stopped waiting for the 33 ms UI tick.

> **Note:** TE sync came off the display. A full-frame transfer takes longer than a refresh, so TE only added waiting, and nothing tears.
