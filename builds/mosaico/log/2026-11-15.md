Eight colour schemes joined White, Warm, Cyan, Magenta and Spectrum under Light.

- **Duos:** Teal+Orange, Blue+Gold, Pink+Cyan, Violet+Lime.
- **Triads:** RGB, CMY, Secondaries, Sunset.

Ring Light blends a scheme around each ring by slope direction. Ink & Light
gives each colour its own light, two opposite or three at 120°. The rim and the
module LEDs share the colours out.

Pinch now goes to 6×, up from 3×. The per-pixel cost doesn't depend on zoom, so
it stays at 33 fps.

Glass Dish and Still Water are gone. The settings store moved to version 5, and
a saved scene that pointed at one of them opens Tone Sweep.
