First app on the Mosaico. Kaleido started as a spec and a clickable prototype, ran in the PC simulator next, and was on the device by evening.

- **Frame pipeline:** a render task pinned to core 1 fills a pool of three PSRAM frame buffers. GSP sends them to the panel from core 0. That took it from about 13 to 21 fps.
- **Sources:** three generative patterns (Lava Cells, Flora, Marbling), images added from a phone, and the camera module. The PPA crops each camera frame to the centre square and scales it to 128×128 in one pass.
- **Looks:** eight presets, from Rose Window to Neon Lattice. L and R step through them, and holding a key changes the source.
- **Phone page:** `kaleido.local` serves a 4.7 KB page for image links, uploads and snapshots. The Function button saves the frame as a 480×480 JPEG.

> **Trap:** I rotated the picture in the GSP presenter and the touch separately, and taps never landed in the right place. The BSP rotates panel and touch together from one setting: 270 with the module at the bottom.
