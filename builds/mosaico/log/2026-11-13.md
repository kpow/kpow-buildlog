Second app on the Mosaico: Chladni, a port of megafun's ChladniModule. It draws
resonant-plate standing waves. 42 (m,n) figures morph into each other while sand
gathers on the nodal lines, in one of eight palettes.

- **Controls:** a tap, the Function button or a shake strikes the plate. R and L step figures. Tilt or drag bends the resonance.
- **Tune rail:** double-tap opens 13 settings, saved in NVS.
- **LEDs:** the Interaction Module shows a turning slice of the plate at 50 Hz.
- **Speed:** the first build was capped at 16-20 fps. Splitting the pixel loop across both cores took it to 38-44 frames a second, above the panel's ~33 fps ceiling.

Each app is its own firmware. To switch, hold AI at power-on to reach Vibe Mode,
open My IDEAs and tap one. An app gets on that grid by installing it over USB,
then tapping Save Current Build.

> **Trap:** On Vibe Mode 0.2.1, Save Current Build failed for every app with
> ESP_ERR_INVALID_ARG (0x102). I guessed it was the 0.3 update, and it was:
> updating Vibe Mode to 0.3.0 fixed it.
