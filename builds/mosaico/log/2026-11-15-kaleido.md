Cathedral and Acid Bloom came over from Cymatics as Kaleido's looks 9 and 10.
On the device they ran at 13 to 17 fps. Their per-pixel tables, about 27 KB,
need fast internal RAM. Kaleido runs Wi-Fi and the camera and had about 22 KB
to spare, so the tables sat in PSRAM: 35 to 60 ms per half frame instead of 7.

| Tried | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Close the camera | It never reopened. Reverted |
| Interleave the tables | No faster from PSRAM |
| Borrow the mirror's texture | 33 fps, Wi-Fi fine |

The mirror looks keep a 64 KB texture in internal RAM that sits idle during
these two looks. They now borrow it and hand it back when a mirror look returns.
Booting straight into one of them used to stop Wi-Fi from starting. Their small
buffers now go to PSRAM explicitly.

> **Dead end:** Closing the camera to free RAM left it timing out on every frame
> after it reopened, until a cold power cycle.
