Added an attract mode: on power-up it plays a self-animating tower demo behind the start screen, replays after two title scrolls, and the game-over screen falls back to it after two passes. It never sits on a dead screen.

The button wiring auto-detects now. At boot the firmware reads the button and treats whatever it sees as "not pressed", so it works with a normally-open or normally-closed switch and I don't have to remember which one I soldered into which wand.
