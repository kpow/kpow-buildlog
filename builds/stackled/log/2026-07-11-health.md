Removed the lives counter and replaced it with the bar-erosion mechanic the game now hangs on: wall-bounces and misses eat the bar's width directly, and width zero is game over. Moved the life pips to a 2px strip above the current row — they were getting buried inside tall stacks. Disabled hold-to-quit and logged button presses to chase input bugs. Then authored the difficulty ramp. The first cut gave thick pieces on the early levels: 3-row blocks on L1, 2-row on L2, single rows from L3. I then softened the opener further to a 4-row-block intro on L1, with thickness stepping down each level to single rows by L4.
