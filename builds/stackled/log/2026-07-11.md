Full bring-up on the 8×32. Flipped both display axes so the tower grows upward, fixed the NC button contact, ran a siren test on the speaker. Then tuning, all of it from watching people play:

- **Lives:** four complete misses a game, with a pip flashed on each one
- **Intensity:** 6 to 10, because at 6 it washes out in a lit room
- **Grace rows:** the first three rows are free, so a fresh player can't lose on the first move
- **Stale presses:** swallowed across state transitions. A leftover press on the game-over screen was skipping the next screen entirely.
