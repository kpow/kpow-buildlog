Swapped in an FC16 4-in-1 module and reconfigured for the real playfield: four MAX7219 8×8 matrices chained into a field 8 wide and 32 tall.

Wiring a fresh chain is fiddly, so the bring-up kept a checkerboard retry loop while I was hot-plugging the matrix on the bench, then went back to the standard loop once the solder held. The button LED moved off GPIO10 to GPIO1, and the pin tables and wiring diagram now match what's actually soldered.
