Swapped in an FC16 4-in-1 module and reconfigured for the real playfield: four MAX7219 8×8 matrices chained into an 8-wide, 32-tall field. Wiring a fresh chain is fiddly, so I kept a checkerboard retry loop in the bring-up for hot-plugging the matrix on the bench, then restored the standard bring-up loop once the solder held. Also moved the button LED off GPIO10 to GPIO1 and updated the pin tables and wiring diagram to match as-built.
