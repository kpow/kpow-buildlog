Scaffold to finished game firmware in one session: engine, level progression, scoring.

The whole field is bits. Each row is a `uint8_t`, and landing a piece is three operations:

- AND the candidate row with the row below, and what survives is the overlap
- popcount that, and you have the new width
- count trailing zeros, and you have the left edge

No pixel arrays anywhere, just bit twiddling on a non-blocking `millis()` state machine. High score goes to NVS so it survives a power cycle.

Wrote the build guide out in full while the wiring was fresh, down to drawing the resistor bodies with correct 5-band colour codes so the BOM reads straight off the page. Renamed the whole thing STACKLED somewhere in there.
