Phase 0 was design only. Four full mockups with fake data, every tile, the launchpad
and the chat panel, nothing built yet. Direction D won: A's layout with a pinch of C,
which means 2px black borders with 4px hard shadows, tilted stickers, one hand-drawn
squiggle and the DigitalOcean bill as a price tag.

Then the shell. Hono on 127.0.0.1:4040, SQLite in Application Support, collectors on
timers. Two tiles went live and the rest sat as placeholders in their final positions:

- **Launchpad:** last git commit or newest file per app, a dusty sticker after 30 days
  untouched, and a live running check for the local servers.
- **Hosted:** HTTP checks with 24 points of history per site, plus DigitalOcean's apps
  and balance once a read-only token was in the Keychain.

Before any of it, a Python spike against the Bambu P2S confirmed it reports state,
file, percent, time left, temps and AMS trays over LAN MQTT with Developer Mode off.
That answered the one question that could have killed the printing tile later.
