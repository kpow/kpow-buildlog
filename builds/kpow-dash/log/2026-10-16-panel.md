Three changes, all typed into the dash and applied by the dash:

- The ask panel is upside down now. Message box and mode switch sit under the header,
  the thread runs downward beneath them, newest exchange first. The box drags taller
  from its bottom-right corner.
- A row of five day buttons over the to-do input: today, tmrw, then the next three
  weekdays with their dates. Tapping one dates the to-do and overrides a day typed at
  the end of the text.
- The all / dusty / recent chips came off the launchpad header. It always shows dusty
  first anyway, so they were decoration taking up the row.

When a change lands on main the status line says deploying, and a green banner sits
under the chat box until I dismiss it. Watching it edit its own panel while the panel
is open is still a strange thing to sit through.
