The listening tile started on Last.fm and moved to the Spotify Web API, borrowing
spotify-mcp's sign-in. Every play goes into a plays table, so "this week" outlives
Spotify's 50-play window.

Then the throttling showed its teeth. One Spotify app is shared by this dash, the
vizSpot and kpow.xyz, so a 429 any of them earns belongs to all three. The cooldown had
to survive a server restart, and recently-played dropped to every 30 minutes.

The fix was to stop asking. kpow.xyz is now the only thing polling Spotify for history,
and everything else reads its `/api/music/plays` mirror. Once that endpoint was
deployed the direct fallback came out of the dash, and vizbrain went from polling every
minute to every ten.

> **Lesson:** a rate limit is shared by every app behind one set of credentials, so one
> of them has to own the polling and the rest have to read its copy.
