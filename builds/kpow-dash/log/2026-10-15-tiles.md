Every tile is one collector file on its own interval, writing a snapshot the UI reads
later. Nothing here spends a token.

- **Build log:** this repo's `status.mjs` and `builds.json` every 10 minutes, with each
  build's unlogged commits and a "log it" button that copies the slash command.
- **Claude usage:** the transcripts in `~/.claude` every 5 minutes, reading only files
  touched in the last 8 days and reusing the rest. Cost is API-equivalent at list
  prices.
- **3D + printing:** `~/projects/3d` every 10 minutes for FreeCAD, STEP and 3mf counts
  and the newest Bambu Studio plate preview, plus one read-only MQTT link to the P2S.
- **Hardware:** `dns-sd` every 2 minutes, remembering what it has seen so a device that
  drops off reads as offline instead of quietly vanishing.
- **Listening:** recent scrobbles every 2 minutes, the all-time artist list every 6
  hours.

> **Trap:** Bambu Studio will happily print something that was never saved as a .3mf
> into `~/projects/3d`, so the tile has to check the printer's own job history before
> it nags me about slicing.
