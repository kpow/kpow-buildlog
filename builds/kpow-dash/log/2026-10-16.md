One layout was doing two jobs badly, so the tile now has a printing form and an idle
form. While a job runs, a black instrument cluster takes the whole card: percent, time
left, done-at, elapsed, layer count, nozzle and bed against their targets, part and aux
fans, the speed profile as a four-step meter, wifi, all four AMS slots with colour and
remaining, HMS codes, and the plate preview with a layer line drawn across it. Projects
drop to a footer rail. When nothing is printing the machine collapses to one strip and
the projects take the card: four bench cards ordered by what needs doing, then a dense
rack of everything else.

The six-per-page carousel is gone. It hid 13 of 19 projects and lost my place on every
re-render.

Four things the live printer corrected about the design:

- The P2S has no chamber probe, so that gauge is dropped instead of reading "—"
  forever.
- Cloud prints send no `gcode_start_time`, so elapsed and total are derived from
  percent and time left, and labelled as estimates.
- `mc_print_stage` is a coarse 1/2 flag, not the stage. `stg_cur` is.
- There is no layer height in the report, so the plate tag shows the layer count rather
  than a z in mm.

> **Note:** a cloud print's `subtask_name` is the slicer preset, not a filename, so it
> matches no project folder and shows up without a hero image. LAN and SD prints keep
> their real names.
