Desktop OAuth with PKCE for personal and work, with the refresh tokens handed to the
Keychain over stdin rather than written anywhere. Today reads every calendar that is
ticked in Google Calendar's own sidebar, so what lands on the dash is what I already
decided I wanted to see. Inbox shows the unread count per account with the newest
senders and subjects.

To-dos live in SQLite next to the snapshots. A trailing word sets the due date, so
typing "saturday: check vizBrain mac speaker" files itself without a date picker.

The action routes started refusing requests from other web origins the same day. A page
open in another tab has no business posting to a server on localhost.
