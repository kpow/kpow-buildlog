The bench is alive. All 11 modes cycle off the encoder, Steer Cube through Room Radar, with the LEDs running on core 0 and the ST7789 pushed to 80 MHz SPI so nothing tears while a mode is animating.

The matrix effects are ported from NoodleLab.

No WiFi, no Bluetooth, no app. It boots into its own modes and that's the whole contract.
