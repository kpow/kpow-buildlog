The ESP32-S3 bench is alive with all 11 modes cycling off the encoder — Steer Cube, Warp Tunnel, Box Physics, Filaments, Room Radar, and the rest. Here it's rendering Chladni, one of 42 standing-wave figures, on the 2" ST7789. Dual-core with a PSRAM framebuffer, matrix FX ported from noodlez-v2, and no connectivity at all — a fully standalone appliance.
