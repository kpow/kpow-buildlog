Room Radar and Presence Log exist to prove out the HLK-LD2412 mmWave sensor. Both modes just show what the sensor sees: presence in the room, and a rolling log of when it changed.

> **Next:** spin the presence detection out into a vizBot add-on, so the robot knows when someone is nearby. noode-exp is the bench where that gets proven first.
