The Room Radar and Presence Log modes are the test bed for the HLK-LD2412 mmWave sensor. The plan is to spin presence detection out into a vizBot add-on so vizBot can tell when someone is nearby. noode-exp is where the sensor gets proven before it moves onto the robot.
