Folded megafun into the monorepo. All three boards share one core now, and the differences live in capability flags each board declares rather than in forks of the code.

Per-board panel profiles landed with it, plus an on-board tuner, and the original tinyfun board is enrolled as profile M1. The launcher auto-rotates its attract cards after idle. Rewrote the README for three boards with a pre-commit guardrail, because a README about three boards will rot in a week otherwise.
