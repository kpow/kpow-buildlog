The old case was a face module latched onto a tub, with buttons printed as flexure tongues in the face. Those tongues snapped off. v2 is a new enclosure in SolidCraft, generated from one script, with every key dimension a named parameter.

- **Buttons:** three separate square plungers, printed flange-down and trapped from inside by the board.
- **Layout:** portrait, screen at the top, an open thumb rest under the buttons.
- **Stack:** layers that bear on each other: back plate, battery cradle, board tray, board, face.
- **Closure:** a back plate on a dovetail and one M3 screw in the base of a vizBot-style hoop.

Three print tests on the 25th moved the HW-373 charger around until it sat right. Snap tabs pressed on its LEDs, so it ended up hooked under two corner lips with the port hole holding its USB-C. Tightening the screw pressed the buttons until the cradle stopped short of the divider.
