The first v2 print passed on the 28th: everything fit, the buttons worked and the back plate didn't squeeze. A drop of foam tape under the battery made it snug. Then I tried to make it smaller, for real or just to the eye, without moving any internal pocket.

The trim version bevels the sides and thumb end at 45° and slims the hoop. The shell went from 12,602 to 10,125 mm³. It feels great in the hand. Fixes followed the same day:

- **Battery leads:** the cradle's lead hole became an open slot, so a pre-wired battery drops in.
- **Cradle:** 1 mm shorter. Tightening the screw had bound the buttons again.
- **Charge port:** 1 mm toward the back (trim-v4, not printed yet).

On the software side, the doubling cube reached tinyFun: hold OK+R to double, then a TAKE / DROP sheet. It's committed as WIP and not yet checked on device.
