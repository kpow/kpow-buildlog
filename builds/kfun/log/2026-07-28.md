First working console: a five-game handheld on the LOLIN S3 Mini Pro driving a
128×128 panel. Brought up the boot screen and a custom typeface, and got CANYON — a
3D anti-gravity racer — running on the panel. Also did an Asteroids pass: added a
3-shot spread and lightened the opening wave so the first screen isn't a full field
of rocks.
