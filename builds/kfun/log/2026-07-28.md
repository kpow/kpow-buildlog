First working console: a five-game handheld on the LOLIN S3 Mini Pro, 128×128, three buttons.

Boot screen and a custom typeface up, and SkyGrid's ancestor running on the panel as CANYON, a 3D anti-gravity racer. Gave Asteroids a 3-shot spread and thinned out the opening wave, because a full field of rocks on a 128 px screen is not a first level, it's a wall.
