Added a VS PLAYER mode to Backgammon over an ESP-NOW link, built up phase by phase —
turn transport first, then game-over sync, then the doubling cube — mirroring the
megafun implementation. Followed it with a per-device mirror and a resilience layer
so a dropped packet doesn't desync the board, keeping mega and bigfun at parity.
