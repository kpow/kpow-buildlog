My first PCB project, and my first time in KiCad. The goal: replace tinyfun's LOLIN S3 Mini Pro with a board that takes a 1.3" 240×240 ST7789 screen, a d-pad plus a fifth OK button, an amp and speaker, and the vibration motor.

Two boards came out of it:

| | Rev A | Module carrier M1 |
|---|---|---|
| Size | 32 × 65 mm | 34 × 70 mm |
| Build | JLC assembles the SMD side | Hand-soldered modules |
| Power | TP4056 + DW01A + ideal diode | TP4056 module + Schottky |

Rev A came first. Then I realised it wasn't the board I had in mind. I build five or six tinyfuns at a time to give away, and the hand wiring is the worst part. M1 is a plain carrier: a SuperMini, a TP4056 module, a MAX98357A breakout and my haptic module drop onto marked outlines. I left the IMU off it. I barely used it.

Both pass KiCad's DRC with zero errors. Neither is ordered yet.
