Started the v2 sprint with four goals: modern type, backgammon match play, tinyFun on the ESP-NOW link, and a new 2-player game. Three phases landed on day one.

- **Type:** Slackey for titles and the boot wordmark, Kenney Pixel 8 for small text, kenvector for numbers. The old small sizes were blurry at 0.85" and R read as A. Checked on glass on a profile-B board.
- **Chords:** three buttons can't double or drop a cube, so two-button chords, holds and a SHIFT layer now sit on one shared ButtonCore. Every timed gesture shows a filling ring. A chord-lab screen settled the window at 120 ms, about 98% on device.
- **Link:** receive moved onto the loop task, peers filtered by MAC, a CRC-32 state hash, protocol v2 with a version-mismatch screen. 200 lossy matches run headless in the tests.
