Restructured everything into the kFun monorepo — a shared common/ with tinyfun/ and
bigfun/ on top — and got the full app booting the shared console on the SuperMini.
Then a round of art and effects work on the wide board: landscape backgammon with
traditional triangle points, a centred Tetris well with side panels, an Arkanoid
rebuild, a sprite pipeline off the Kenney CC0 art, launcher attract cards, nebula
backgrounds with post-FX, and a themed game-over screen.
