Restructured into the kFun monorepo, a shared `common/` with `tinyfun/` and `bigfun/` as thin board ports on top, and got the full app booting on the SuperMini.

Then art and effects for the wide board, which is where the two consoles start being different games:

- backgammon in landscape, with traditional triangle points
- Tetris centred, with side panels either side of the well
- Breakout rebuilt as Arkanoid
- a sprite pipeline off the Kenney CC0 art
- attract cards on the launcher, nebula backgrounds with post-FX, a themed game-over screen
