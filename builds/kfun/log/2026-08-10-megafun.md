megafun was a way to keep expanding on what I was doing. I'd seen a tilted plane used as the support for a screen, and I had a small joystick lying around, so the two went together.

I wanted a marbles game, and you can't do marbles without a joystick. It led to exploring a lot of other things on the same base:

- **Marbles:** the tilt maze that started it
- **Chladni:** a resonant-plate pattern generator
- **LED Studio:** a menu for driving the LED cap on top
