Ordered 20 module carriers from JLC. Their economic assembly fits the 12 small parts: five buttons, six 1206 passives and the B5819W Schottky. I solder the modules myself, all from AliExpress. About $250 for 20 tinyFuns.

Before paying I had an agent review the order files. It caught three things:

- **Solder paste:** the gerber zip had no paste layer, so there was nothing to cut a stencil from.
- **Placement origin:** the placement file and the gerbers used different origins.
- **Amp SD pad:** it sat under the TP4056's USB shell. It's now a no-connect, and the amp board's own pull-up keeps it in mono.

The backlight also moved off 3V3. Left on, it would drain the 500 mAh cell in about a day. It now runs from GPIO8, so firmware can turn it off.

Only 9 "big chip" MAX98357A boards were left, so the rest are the small-chip version. The speaker is a 1609: 16 × 9 mm, 8 Ω.

There's an interactive 3D model to spin, explode and take apart: https://kpow.xyz/tinyfun-m1/index.html
