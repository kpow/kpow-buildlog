Ported the console to a new m5bigfun target — CoreS3 with the Faces Bottom3 and a
Gamepad3 — and lit its LED strips: effects are ranked by tier, every game gets a
voice on the strips, and Backgammon shows whose turn it is. Topped it with a
top-level chooser so one device can hold two firmwares and pick between them at boot.
