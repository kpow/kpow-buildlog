Ported the console to m5bigfun: a CoreS3 sitting in a Faces Bottom3 with a Gamepad3, and two rows of five SK6812 to play with.

The LEDs got a job rather than a glow. Effects are ranked by tier, every game has a voice on the strips, and Backgammon uses them to show whose turn it is.

Since the device also holds the vizBot firmware, there's a top-level chooser in the factory partition now: one board, two firmwares, pick at boot.
