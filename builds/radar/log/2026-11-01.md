The first one fully assembled in the v2 case, and a name for the gift box: tinyradar.

- **Rename:** the hotspot is TinyRadar-Setup and the page is tinyradar.local. Saved settings survive the rename.
- **Boot:** with a location saved it goes straight to the radar. Hold the range button for a second to see the web address.
- **No location yet:** the radar still comes up, rings and sweep with no planes, plus a small "Set your location" card.
- **Range button:** a press on GPIO 6 or 7 counts, so either wiring works.
