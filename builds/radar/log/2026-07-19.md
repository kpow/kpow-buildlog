The browser side got real. There's an interactive touch radar served straight off the device now, gzipped into flash, pulling traffic from `/api/flights` and letting the browser enrich each plane with route and photo data from adsbdb and planespotters. Per-device themes, and a header with live context so you know what you're looking at. The heap fix is the part that mattered: serving the SPA and running the live ADS-B poll were fighting over the same RAM.

Then the TTP223 touch pad, which had started cycling range on its own at about two steps a second. A loose sensor makes the line oscillate, and every phantom step also wrote NVS and forced a refetch. Three defenses:

1. A press only counts after the level holds for 40 ms. Real touches last 50 ms and up; noise edges don't.
2. More than 6 taps in 8 seconds locks the input out until it's been quiet for 10. The BOOT button and the web UI stay live.
3. Range persists to NVS once, 3 seconds after it stops changing.
