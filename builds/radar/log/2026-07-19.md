Big day for the browser side. Built an **interactive touch radar web UI** served straight off the device, with per-device themes and — the important part — a **heap fix** so serving the SPA doesn't collide with the live ADS-B poll. The app is gzipped and baked into flash; it hits `/api/flights` for the traffic and lets the browser enrich each plane with route and photo data. Added a page header that shows live context so you know what you're looking at without hunting.

On the hardware side, **hardened the TTP223 range-touch input** against a flapping line — the capacitive pad was chattering and cycling range on its own, so I debounced it into behaving. Also swept the repo: stopped tracking `.DS_Store` junk and told git to ignore the capsule-radar reference project I'd been reading for ideas.
