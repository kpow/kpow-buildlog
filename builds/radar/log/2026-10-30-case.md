A few of these are going out as Christmas presents, and the old case was the slow part. It was a kit of STEP parts that took a lot of assembly and a lot of superglue. The v2 redesign was modelled parametrically in SolidCraft.

| | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Printed pieces | 86 | 4 |
| Glue joints | about 80 | 1 |
| Assembly | 60–90 min | about 8 min |

- **Body:** shell, deck, button recess and keyboard tray in one white print, about 50 g, so it prints with no purge waste.
- **Faceplate:** printed face down on the AMS with a black bezel and grey ring and rivets. Two M3 screws from the back hold it.
- **Keyboard insert:** 55 keys printed in place in four colours. It's the one glued part.
- **Display clip:** a 1 g part that holds the round LCD in place of the old M2 screws and spacers.

The TTP223 touch pad is gone. It was finicky and gave no feedback, so range is now a 12 mm tactile switch that drops into a recess in the deck.

The first test print changed a few things. The board now mounts components-up, so its LED shows. The body grew 10 mm taller and 5 mm deeper, to 76 × 63 mm.

> **Note:** module listings for the GC9A01 disagree on depth. Measure the glass-to-PCB stack before printing the clip.
