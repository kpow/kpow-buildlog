Started from a modern-SDK fork of a plane-radar project on the ESP32-C3 and then gutted most of it. The stock build locked up every so often: it fired a burst of ADS-B requests and buffered each whole response body. One request on a backoff, plus a stream parse with a field filter so the body is never held whole, and the lockups stopped.

The rest was one long session:

- **Dual core:** the network fetch on its own task, so a poll never stalls a frame
- **Interpolation:** targets dead-reckon between the 3-second polls instead of teleporting
- **ESP32-S3 Super Mini:** a second target, for the better radio, with WiFi retry hardening
- **Looks:** 16-bit colour at 80 MHz SPI, a sweep with a comet trail, 30 fps pacing

By the end it was a live scope sitting in the printed slatted cube: round LCD, green grid, real callsigns.
