An experimental side branch: all six tinyradars turn into one set of eyes, steered by an M5StickC Plus with a Mini JoyC hat. It's a hidden mode in its own build. The gift firmware doesn't have it, and the radars go back to tracking planes when the eyes switch off.

- **Rendering:** every pixel is drawn into the radar's strip buffer on both cores. Lookup tables and fast-math tricks took a frame from 142 ms to about 50.
- **Radio:** ESP-NOW. With the eyes on, the radars drop WiFi and sit on one fixed channel with the remote. Before that, the eyes stalled whenever the remote settled on an overlapping channel.
- **Styles:** living hazel, cartoon, flame iris, goat and LED matrix are in the video. Radar and camera-aperture eyes exist but were left out.

> **Trap:** the Mini JoyC locks up, holding the I2C clock low, if you read it with a repeated start or ask for too many bytes. Write the register, send STOP, then read exactly two bytes.
