A gift sprint: the radar has to work for someone in another city who never touches a USB cable.

- **Real map underlay:** the faint line map is replaced by a real basemap. The browser renders it from OpenFreeMap tiles for the device's location and uploads it to a flash partition. The device memory-maps it, so it costs no heap. It comes in Day and Dusk themes. A 12-minute soak on the polish build logged 228 polls, 0 fetch errors and a 44.6 KB worst-case free heap.
- **OTA with rollback:** this S3 turned out to have 8 MB of flash, which leaves room for a second 3 MB app slot. Updates stream into the spare slot with no RAM buffer. A new image runs on probation and is kept after 30 s healthy on Wi-Fi. It rolls back if it never gets healthy in 5 minutes or crashes on boot. An interrupted upload, both rollback paths and map survival were all tested on the radar. A `v*` tag now builds the release files.
- **Range button:** the TTP223 touch pad is replaced by a tactile switch on GPIO 6, read by an interrupt so fast presses step 1-for-1. A flapping line locks it out after more than 16 presses in 2 seconds.
- **S3 only:** C3 support is removed. CI passes on main again for the first time since July, now that the airport-data drift check is a warning instead of a failure.

A redesigned device web page is in review in its own PR.
