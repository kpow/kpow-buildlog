Radar had started coming up empty — grid, sweep, no planes. Turned out the ADS-B endpoint was returning its response with **chunked transfer encoding**, and my stream parser was choking on the chunk-size markers instead of the JSON. De-chunked the body before parsing and the traffic came right back. Sneaky one: the fetch succeeded, the heap was fine, it was just quietly feeding garbage to the parser.
