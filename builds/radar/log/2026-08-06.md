Both scopes got a faint map underlay: coast, lakes, rivers, roads, dim enough to place the traffic without competing with it. A regional crop of Natural Earth 1:10m bakes into flash at about 57 KB, projects once per range change into a fixed 14 KB screen-space cache, and draws under the sweep. No heap, so the TLS headroom is untouched. Now you can see a plane following a river valley.

Building it turned up an old bug. The device projection was missing its `cos(lat)`, so everything was stretched about 26% east-west, and the device and the web app had been quietly disagreeing the whole time. Both read from one shared projection header now.

Routes learned to handle multi-leg trips too. Every stop renders, `JFK → RIC → SFO`, with the leg the aircraft is actually flying picked by smallest detour against its live position and brightened.
