Added a faint **map underlay** to both scopes — coast, lakes, rivers, roads — drawn dim enough to give a sense of place without fighting the traffic on top. It makes the whole thing legible: you can tell a plane is following a river valley or lined up on a road.

Also taught the route display to handle **multi-leg trips**: it now shows every stop on the route and highlights the leg the aircraft is currently flying, instead of collapsing everything to origin-and-destination. (Refreshed the README a couple of days earlier while I was in here.)
