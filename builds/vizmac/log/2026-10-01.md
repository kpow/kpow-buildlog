Pushing the knob goes up one level everywhere in the controller UI. Modes is the top, with nothing above it, so push did nothing there. It now opens the highlighted mode instead, guarded by 500 ms on screen or any turn of the knob, so mashing push to get back up to Modes still stops at Modes. Added a push hint on the screen, and corrected the navigation comment in the source, which still described an older arrangement of the buttons.
