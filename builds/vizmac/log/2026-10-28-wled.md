A light's page on the controller now opens with a live view of its LEDs: a 32x8 wall, a 16x16 matrix or a strip drawn as a ribbon. The same frame is resampled onto the 16x8 panels.

WLED's `/json/live` is turned off on these builds, so the peek uses the WebSocket live view that WLED's own UI uses. The light streams its pixels only while its page is open. That breaks the render-on-device rule on purpose: the controller cannot run WLED's effects itself.

- **Palettes:** names from the light, gradient swatches from `/json/palx`, loaded a page at a time.
- **Speed and intensity:** next to brightness, edited in a bottom sheet with the peek still in view.
- **All lights:** a page for brightness, preset by name, palette and effect across every light.
