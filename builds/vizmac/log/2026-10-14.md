title: "Modes cards slide instead of swapping"
tags: [controller, ui]
media: []
---
Turning the knob on Modes now slides. The old card leaves one side and the new one comes in from the other, ease-out cubic over 260 ms. Turning right brings the next card in from the right, where its peek already sits.

Nothing is redrawn mid-slide. The old frame is held in a 150 KB PSRAM buffer, and each step pushes the card band of both frames, offset, straight to the panel. Page dots and hints stay put, and a turn part-way through carries on from what is on screen. Without the buffer the cards just swap, the way they did before.
