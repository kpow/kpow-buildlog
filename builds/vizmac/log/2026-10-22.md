title: "The controller went quiet: a socket that swallowed its errors"
tags: [controller, bugfix, networking]

---
The macro pad lost its audio and keypress sync. vizMac had started at login on Oct 18 with a UDP socket that couldn't reach the LAN, and the sync sender swallowed the error, so the controller never got a sync packet and rendered with audio off. A restart fixed it.

The sender now records each controller's last send error, logs it and shows it in the web UI's Controller card, along with where to turn on Local Network access. On a failed send it opens a fresh socket, at most every 5 seconds.
