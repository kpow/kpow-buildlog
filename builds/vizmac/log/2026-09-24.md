Got the ROCCAT Vulcan Pro TKL taking per-key colors from the Mac in one day, built in layers and tested on the keyboard after each one:

- **Driver:** USB HID direct mode, with the onboard lighting backed up and restored on every exit. The key map was checked key by key; Menu and Fn are swapped compared to OpenRGB.
- **Effects:** a fixed-rate engine on an x/y key grid, the built-ins, and 17 Noodle 2K effects compiled unchanged from noodlez-v2.
- **Inputs:** keypresses from the keyboard's own HID reports, and Mac system audio through a Core Audio process tap, analyzed the same way as the Noodle audio sat.
- **App:** vizKeys.app with a skull in the menu bar, a web UI with palette swatches, start at login, and reconnects after unplug or sleep.

It started as kpowRGBkey and became vizKeys the same day. The plan for an ESP32-S3 desk controller went in last: a knob, a 2.4" screen and two 8x8 panels as a live preview.

> **Trap:** the keyboard rejects direct mode unless the lighting packet sent just before it is in mode 0x09. Echoing the stored profile only worked while the profile happened to be in that mode. When it flipped to static, vizKeys said "connected" and the keys kept their onboard lighting. vizKeys now forces 0x09 and writes the original back on exit.
