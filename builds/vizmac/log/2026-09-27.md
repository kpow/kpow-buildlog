The controller sits in the best spot on the desk, but it was only used for picking effects. It now has four modes behind a card-style mode picker: Keys, Now Playing, Clock and WLED. Each mode keeps running and opens where it was left. After a restart the controller comes back in the last one.

The buttons took three tries. The final scheme:

- **Knob push:** back, fired the moment it goes down. A few pushes always land on the mode picker.
- **Knob hold (2 s):** sleep.
- **KO:** choose. **KO hold:** the mode's shortcut, like Tune in Keys.

Boost is gone and always on. A Keyboard LEDs setting keeps the keyboard preview on the 16×8 in any mode.

> **Lesson:** A raw button log over UDP showed every "missed" press arrived. They were slow presses held past the 0.6 s hold time. It also caught a real bug: a one-count wiggle while pushing counted as a turn. A turn now needs 3 counts past the detent.
