tags: [audio, bugfix]

The audio effects were logging an invalid-value warning from numpy on every frame. A non-finite sample from the systemaudio helper poisoned the FFT, and the band normalise then divided inf by inf. Non-finite samples are scrubbed to silence before analysis now, and both decaying peaks are floored so the divisor is never zero.
