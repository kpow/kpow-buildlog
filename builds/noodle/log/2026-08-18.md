title: "Killed a FeatherS3 with 48 pixels"
tags: [hardware-lesson, power]

Killed a FeatherS3. The 12×4's 5 V and ground were on the Feather's own pins, and 48 WS2812s at full brightness ask about 2.9 A from a rail good for roughly one. It browned out under bright frames for a while, then died, and wouldn't enumerate again even in ROM bootloader mode.

The wing is physically stuck on the Feather's rail, so that one is capped in software instead. Everything else draws from the PSU.

> **Trap:** `FastLED.setMaxPowerInVoltsAndMilliamps()` will not save you here. It applies one global budget and has no idea two strips sit on different supplies, so it looks like protection and isn't.
