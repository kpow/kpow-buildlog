Stripped the rig to a bare MCU and brought it back subsystem by subsystem. Things that did not come back: the icon sat, and later the 8×12, with the 16×16 moving onto a console pin instead.

What changed on the way:

- **SparkFun inputs** onto their own I2C bus, because the CAP1203 was picking up EMI from the Adafruit bus and reporting touches nobody made
- **Screensaver** replaced with a live Chladni resonant-plate visualiser
- **UI** redone on an anti-aliased chrome kit, which finally killed the flicker

A rebuild is the only time you get to un-choose things. I took it.
