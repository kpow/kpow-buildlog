Added an external 8×8 and ran out of pins. Between the TFT, two I2C buses, the local matrix, the wing and the filament lanes, the FeatherS3 had nothing usable left.

The fix could have been a bigger chip or an expander. Instead every LED surface becomes its own ESP-NOW satellite, with its own board and its own render loop, taking effect state over the air.

> **Lesson:** when you're out of pins, stop counting pins. The system scales by adding boards now, and I've never hit the wall again.
