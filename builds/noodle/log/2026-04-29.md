The first satellite: a separate board with a mic, running spectral-flux beat detection with a bass lean, broadcasting audio features over peered ESP-NOW.

Every effect on every board reads the same bass, mid, treble and beat, so the whole rig moves on the same downbeat instead of each surface guessing on its own.
