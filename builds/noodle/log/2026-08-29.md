A content wave, and a name.

The effect catalog went to 21 with a trimmed 2D particle engine and the WLED particle effects on top of it, plus a real 16-band GEQ analyzer. Then the palette system: one global palette every effect renders through, with host-parity tests proving palette 0 reproduces the old look exactly, and a picker on the dial.

Rebranded the rig Noodle 2K, with a new layered power-on intro to match. Also brought up a hub75 MatrixPortal satellite doing native 64×32, which is four times the pixels of anything else on the tray.
