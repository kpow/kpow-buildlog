Reworked the motion. The old version slid one section out and back like a tray, and sometimes nothing actually changed on screen. Now every line moves at once while part of the grid rebuilds and a colour block slides to a new cell.

That turned up a bug where remixing the whole canvas skipped moving the lines at all.

The Color knob is one smooth sweep now, built on the spacing between Mondrian's own red, blue and yellow, with his palette sitting in the middle of the range.

Flashed it to the rig and every colour was wrong. Took me a minute to notice the global palette tint was still switched on, quietly re-mapping a pattern that brings its own colours.
