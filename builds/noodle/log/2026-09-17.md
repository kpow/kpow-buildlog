Reworked the motion. It used to slide one section out and back like a tray, and sometimes nothing actually changed. Now every line moves at once while part of the grid rebuilds and a color block slides to a new cell. That turned up a bug where remixing the whole canvas skipped moving the lines at all. The Color knob is now one smooth sweep built on the spacing of Mondrian's own red, blue and yellow, with his palette in the middle. Flashed it to the rig and the colors were off until I noticed the palette tint was still switched on.
