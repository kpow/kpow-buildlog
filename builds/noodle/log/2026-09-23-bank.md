Pulled six more patterns off the Patternflow wall: Fractal Storm, Branched Flow, Hypno Circles, Tri March, Vector Field Flow and Pattern 0522. Hypno Circles only exists as JavaScript, so that one is a hand port.

The importer had been seeing 44 of the wall's 194 patterns. The site caps a page at 44 whatever you ask for, and the loop read a short page as the end.

The bank is at 51 slots and still growing, which a grid on a round screen can't hold. The dial got a BANK mode instead: the knob scrolls a list, a tap launches, and once something is playing the knob does nothing. That also killed the old bug where a fast spin skipped patterns. Flashed the dial, Console and hub75, and the panel still lights with 14 KB more static RAM.

> **Next:** the rotation playlist is a 64-bit mask, so 64 slots is the ceiling until the protocol changes.
