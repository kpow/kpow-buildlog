Stood up the real effects engine: a descriptor table with 10 effects to start, so adding one is a function and a row rather than a new branch in the render loop.

A 4×8 NeoPixel FeatherWing went on mirroring the matrix, and the filaments got a TPS61169 driver with gamma correction so a dim setting actually looks dim.
