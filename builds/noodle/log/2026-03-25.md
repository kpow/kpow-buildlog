First pass at the idea: one controller driving a row of LED grids and filaments, built on a breadboard with a rat's nest of jumpers behind it.

It worked, barely. Every new surface meant more wires and more flakiness, and I could see the ceiling from where I was standing. Tore it down and went looking for a sturdier way to prototype, which turned out to be Adafruit's STEMMA QT modules.
