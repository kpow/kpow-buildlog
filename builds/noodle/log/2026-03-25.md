First pass at the idea: one controller driving a series of LED grids and
filaments. Built it on a breadboard with a rat's nest of wires. It worked, barely,
but it was obvious this would never scale — every new surface meant more jumpers
and more flakiness. Tore it down and went looking for a more solid way to
prototype (landed on Adafruit STEMMA QT modules).
