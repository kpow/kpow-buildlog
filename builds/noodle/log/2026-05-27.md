Added a motorized disco ball as its own satellite: stepper, spot LED, OLED, and a dedicated live target on the console.

The first version ran the stepper off main power and made the ground filthy. Noise bled through the whole Noodle, and it read like a data problem until I looked at what I'd just plugged in. Its own supply fixed it.

> **Trap:** a motor and your logic on one ground rail. Classic, and I still walked into it.
