Three NeoPixel rotary encoders, each owning a slot on whatever target is live. Slots are assigned by I2C address, highest address takes slot 0, so the wiring order decides the layout and nothing has to be configured.

Added a Source Map for assigning a source per surface, and retired Pixelblaze in favour of the native engine. Running two effect engines was one more than the rig needed.
