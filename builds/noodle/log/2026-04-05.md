Rebuilt onto a FeatherS3, and got further in one sitting than the breadboard managed in a week:

- display, encoder and buttons alive
- a sensor dashboard for distance, light and the onboard NeoPixel
- WLED control
- a direct 4×12 matrix running an 8×8 icon slideshow

Solderless modules on one cable is the whole reason. Nothing came loose all day.
