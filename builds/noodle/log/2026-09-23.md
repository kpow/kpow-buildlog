Started from a canvas sketch of Bauhaus rectangles zooming inward and ported it to the Patternflow bank as Nested Frames, bank 44. Named it Twisted Tunnel for the Patternflow wall.

The sketch claimed to loop seamlessly and didn't. Every five seconds each ring took on its neighbour's colour and shape. Keying every ring on its own index fixed it.

Then it drifted a long way from the original:

- **Tilt:** a twist keyed on ring size, so the frames form a spiral that turns as it falls.
- **Colour:** the red, blue and yellow from the Mondrian pattern, with true black and white.
- **Randomness:** each ring gets its own spacing, lean, shape and slow drift.
- **Fills:** about one ring in five carries dots, a grid, stripes or a checker.

I dropped the yellow square the sketch kept fixed at the centre. It looked weird sitting still while everything else moved.

> **Trap:** one float for zoom depth loses most of each frame's step near its wrap at the lowest speed, and the zoom stutters. Split it into a whole part and a fraction.
